/ How we work

Five steps. One point of contact throughout.

  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You tell us what you need — or what you have to offer. We ask direct questions to understand the deal, the timeline and your constraints.

  2. 02

    Qualification

    We assess whether the opportunity is viable and whether we have the right counterparties in our network. We are honest when something is not right for us.

  3. 03

    Matching

    We identify and approach the relevant party on your behalf. No cold blasts — we make targeted, informed introductions.

  4. 04

    Negotiation

    We stay in the room — helping both sides reach terms that hold. We do not disappear once the introduction is made.

  5. 05

    Closure

    We see the transaction through to a signed agreement or formal order. Our fee is tied to completion, not to activity.
